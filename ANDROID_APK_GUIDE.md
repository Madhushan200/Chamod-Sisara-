## 🎯 3 Easy Ways to Get & Install the APK

### 🥇 Method 1: 1-Click Online APK Download (Easiest — No Setup Needed)
1. Deploy your site to Vercel (or any HTTPS URL).
2. Go to **[PWABuilder.com](https://www.pwabuilder.com/)**.
3. Enter your live URL and click **Start**.
4. Click **Package for Android** $\to$ Download the generated **`.apk`** file.
5. Send the `.apk` file to hotel staff devices via WhatsApp, Email, or Google Drive for direct 1-tap installation!

---

### 🥈 Method 2: Open in Android Studio (Native Build)
If you have **Android Studio** installed on your PC:
1. Open Android Studio.
2. Select **Open** $\to$ Choose the folder:  
   `c:\Users\ME\.gemini\antigravity-ide\scratch\wedding-chamod-sisara\android`
3. Wait for Gradle sync to complete.
4. Click **Build** menu $\to$ **Build Bundle(s) / APK(s)** $\to$ **Build APK(s)**.
5. Android Studio will generate the compiled `.apk` in:  
   `android/app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`

---

### 🥉 Method 3: Instant Android WebAPK Install (No Download Needed)
1. Open the URL in Google Chrome on any Android phone.
2. An automatic banner will appear: **"Add ME Colombo to Home screen"** (or tap $\vdots$ $\to$ **"Install App"**).
3. Android creates a native **WebAPK** package that lives in the Android App Drawer and home screen with notification badges and full-screen display.
